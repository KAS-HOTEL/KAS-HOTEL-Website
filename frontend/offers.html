01 / Earn & expire

Earning and expiry

  • Points are added only after check-out and full payment.
  • Gold earns 2%; Platinum earns 5%; Classic 0%.
  • Each earning is valid for exactly 3 months from when it is added, Vietnam time.
  • When you use points, those closest to expiry go first.
02 / Use & release

Using and returning points

  • Each booking can use points for up to 50% of the room amount after vouchers and the member discount.
  • Points are held when the booking is confirmed; they are deducted only after check-out and full payment.
  • Cancelling returns points with their original expiry; points already expired when returned expire straight away.
03 / Refund & balance

Refunds and points owed

  • A refunded booking has the points it earned taken back from that booking.
  • If those points were already used, the amount used is recorded as points owed.
  • Points owed are settled from your next earnings.
  • Your points balance is never negative.
Held points versus deducted points: When you confirm a booking, the points you chose to use are held. Only after the stay is completed and paid in full are they actually deducted. If you cancel, the points come back but their expiry is not extended.

Membership policy

How it applies

01

Joining

Create an account on the KAS website to receive the Classic tier free of charge. Each member has their own code in the form KAS-XXXXXXXXXXXX, which never changes and is never reissued; you can find it in your profile.

Guests without an account book as guests. If they join later, earlier stays do not count towards the tier review and earn no points.

02

Identifying completed bookings

A booking qualifies for the tier review only if it was made on the KAS website while signed in to a member account, and the guest has completed the stay and checked out. Bookings made by phone with the hotel, or made without signing in to a member account, do not count. Cancelled bookings and no-shows do not count.

Each valid booking counts as 1 booking, whatever the number of nights or rooms. The quarter follows the check-out date in Vietnam.

03

Tier thresholds and review

Classic: the default tier. Gold: from 15 completed bookings in a quarter. Platinum: from 30 completed bookings in a quarter. These thresholds count only valid bookings as defined under “Identifying completed bookings”.

Your tier is upgraded right after the check-out that reaches the threshold, but that booking still earns points at your previous tier. The new tier applies only from the next booking and is not backdated. Members keep their tier until the end of the current quarter; at the start of each quarter, the tier is reviewed against the bookings of the previous quarter, never below Classic.

04

Stay privileges and discount

Every tier can check in up to 2 hours early, check out up to 2 hours late and cancel free of charge until 12:00 noon on the check-in day.

Booking direct with KAS gives 10% off the room amount before taxes and fees. Order of application: voucher → 10% member discount → points. Each booking can use at most 1 voucher.

05

Earning points and expiry

Conversion: 1 point = 1 VND when deducted from a booking; points cannot be exchanged for cash. Classic earns no points back, Gold earns 2% and Platinum 5%.

Points are calculated on the room amount actually paid after vouchers and the member discount, excluding taxes and fees, and rounded down. They are added only once the guest has completed the stay and paid in full. Each earning is valid for exactly 3 months, Vietnam time; points closest to expiry are used first.

06

Using points and cancellations

Each booking can use points to pay for up to 50% of the room amount after vouchers and the member discount; points do not apply to taxes and fees.

Points are held when the booking is confirmed and deducted only after the stay is completed and paid in full. If you cancel, the points come back with their original expiry; points already past expiry when returned expire straight away.

07

Refunds and points owed

When a booking is refunded, the points it earned are taken back. If the member has already used them, the amount used is recorded as points owed and settled from the next earnings.

The member points balance is never negative.

08 / Good to know

Frequently
asked questions.

Quick answers to common situations when joining and using KAS points.

Is there a fee to become a KAS member?+
No. When you create an account on the KAS website, you automatically become a Classic member free of charge and receive your own member code in your profile.
I reached Gold after check-out. Does the stay I just finished earn 2%?+
No. The booking that takes you to the Gold threshold earns points at your tier before the upgrade. Gold applies from your next booking and is not backdated.
How long are KAS points valid?+
Each earning is valid for exactly 3 months from when it is added, Vietnam time. When you use points, the batch closest to expiry is used first.
What happens if I cancel after using points?+
The points held are returned with their original expiry. If they have already expired when returned, they expire straight away and are not extended.
Can points owed make my balance negative?+
No. If points from a refunded booking were already used, KAS records them as points owed and settles them from new earnings. The balance shown is never negative.
