THE KAS CITY GUIDE

Four neighbourhood stories.

Each guide connects two KAS hotels with a larger set of real places nearby. Explore the landmarks, food, culture, shopping and nightlife that surround the KAS collection.

01 — The City at Your Doorstep

Start with the city.

Markets, parks, historic landmarks and the central streets of Saigon.

02 — The Taste of Saigon

Eat your way through the city.

Local food, coffee, markets and the streets that give Saigon its flavour.

03 — The Slow Side

Leave room for wandering.

Architecture, museums, shopping and quiet streets.

04 — Saigon After Dark

Stay out a little later.

Walking streets, evening culture, city views and late-night energy.
