KAS Membership

Member policy

Every KAS account is a membership. Tiers follow completed bookings in each calendar quarter.

Silver

Default tier for every new account.

  • 10% member discount on room rates
  • Early check-in / late check-out up to 2 hours
  • Free cancellation until 12:00 on check-in day
  • Cashback: 0%

Gold

15 completed bookings in a calendar quarter.

  • 10% member discount on room rates
  • Early check-in / late check-out up to 2 hours
  • Free cancellation until 12:00 on check-in day
  • Cashback: 2% in KAS points

Platinum

30 completed bookings in a calendar quarter.

  • 10% member discount on room rates
  • Early check-in / late check-out up to 2 hours
  • Free cancellation until 12:00 on check-in day
  • Cashback: 5% in KAS points

Upgrading and keeping your tier

  • A completed booking is a stay that has checked out. Cancelled bookings and no-shows do not count.
  • You move up as soon as a checkout reaches the next threshold. New benefits apply from that moment and are not backdated.
  • You keep your tier for the rest of the quarter. At the start of each quarter your tier is reviewed against the completed bookings of the previous quarter, never below Silver.

KAS points

  • 1 point = 1 VND off a future KAS booking. Points cannot be exchanged for cash.
  • Cashback is calculated on the room amount paid after vouchers and the member discount, excluding taxes and fees, rounded down.
  • Points are added after a completed stay that has been paid in full.
  • Points are valid for 3 calendar months from the day they are earned.

Your current tier and points are shown in My KAS.
