03 / Tier review

Every check-out.
A new milestone.

KAS records each completed booking, moves you up when you reach a threshold and reviews your tier at the start of each quarter.

Upgrade on the spot

Reach the threshold today,
enjoy your new tier from the next booking.

A new tier applies from the moment you meet its requirement. Its benefits do not apply to bookings completed before the upgrade. The booking that takes you over the threshold earns points at your previous tier.

Calendar quarter: Quarter I (January–March), Quarter II (April–June), Quarter III (July–September) and Quarter IV (October–December). Your tier is reviewed on the number of bookings completed in the quarter.

  1. 01 / Check-out

    Booking recorded

    Each booking counts once, on its check-out date in Vietnam, whatever the number of rooms or nights.

  2. 02 / Upgrade

    Move up

    You move up as soon as your completed bookings in the quarter reach the next threshold.

  3. 03 / Quarterly review

    Reviewed each quarter

    You keep your tier for the rest of the quarter. A new quarter reviews it against the previous quarter’s completed bookings; the lowest tier is Classic.

01 / Earn & expire

Earning and expiry

  • Points are added only after check-out and payment in full.
  • Gold earns 2% back; Platinum earns 5% back.
  • Each earning is valid for 3 months from the day it is added, Vietnam time.
  • When you use points, those closest to expiry are used first.
02 / Use & release

Using and returning points

  • Each booking can use points for up to 50% of the room amount after vouchers and the member discount.
  • Points are held when the booking is confirmed and deducted only after check-out and payment in full.
  • A cancelled booking returns its points with their original expiry; points already past expiry when returned expire straight away.
03 / Refund & balance

Refunds and points owed

  • A refunded booking takes back the points it earned.
  • If those points were already used, the amount used is recorded as points owed.
  • Points owed are settled from your next earnings.
  • Your points balance is never negative.

Held and deducted points: When you confirm a booking, the points you chose to use are held. They are deducted only after the stay is completed and paid in full. If you cancel, the points come back, without any extra time added to their expiry.

08 / Good to know

Frequently
asked questions.

Quick answers to common questions about joining and using KAS points.

Is there a fee to become a KAS member?

No. When you create an account on the KAS website, you become a Classic member free of charge and receive your own member code in your profile.

Do bookings made by phone count towards an upgrade?

Yes. Bookings made on the website and by phone with the hotel both count once the guest has completed the stay and paid in full. Each booking counts once, whatever the number of nights or rooms.

I reached Gold on check-out. Does the stay I just finished earn 2%?

No. The booking that takes you to Gold earns points at your tier before the upgrade. Gold benefits apply from your next booking.

How long are KAS points valid?

Each earning is valid for 3 months from the moment it is added, Vietnam time. When you use points, those closest to expiry are used first.

What happens if I cancel after using points?

The points held for the booking come back with their original expiry. Points that have already expired when they come back expire straight away; they are not extended.

Can points owed make my balance negative?

No. If points from a refunded booking were already used, KAS records them as points owed and settles them from your next earnings. Your points balance is never negative.
